Official Speaker Introduction

Dr. Janet M. Maluki is an AI governance and digital trust researcher, cybersecurity specialist, and university lecturer with a PhD in Information Systems and Technology from USIU-Africa. She is an AI Policy Fellow at Lawyers Hub, contributing to African AI governance research, including the Africa AI Governance Index. She also founded CyberBridge Technologies & Solutions, focusing on digital trust, cybersecurity governance, and responsible AI adoption. Her work bridges cybersecurity research, AI policy, and institutional accountability across Africa.
