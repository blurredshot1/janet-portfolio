Official Speaker Introduction

Dr. Janet M. Maluki is an Information Systems and Technology scholar, cybersecurity researcher, university lecturer, and AI governance and digital trust specialist. She holds a PhD in Information Systems and Technology from USIU-Africa, serves as a 2026 Africa AI Policy Fellow, and is developing the CyberBridge Digital Trust Assessment and Management Framework (CDTAM).
