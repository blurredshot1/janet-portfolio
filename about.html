About Me

Scholarship, cybersecurity research, and responsible digital transformation in Africa.

I am an Information Systems and Technology scholar, cybersecurity researcher, university lecturer, and AI governance and digital trust specialist passionate about advancing secure, responsible, and inclusive digital transformation.

I hold a PhD in Information Systems and Technology from the United States International University–Africa (USIU-A), with over seven years of experience in university teaching, research, and technology education.

My research focuses on cybersecurity, machine learning, and intelligent cyber threat detection, particularly enhancing the security of Internet of Things (IoT) networks. My doctoral research explored supervised machine learning, hybrid feature selection, and ensemble learning techniques to improve the accuracy, efficiency, and reliability of cyberattack detection.

Beyond technical cybersecurity research, my professional interests extend to artificial intelligence governance, digital trust, cybersecurity risk management, data protection, and technology policy.

As a 2026 Africa AI Policy Fellow, I contribute to research and policy discussions aimed at strengthening responsible AI governance, institutional readiness, ethics, and inclusion across Africa.

I am developing the CyberBridge Digital Trust Assessment and Management Framework (CDTAM), a practical approach to assessing organizational digital maturity, cybersecurity resilience, governance effectiveness, and readiness for responsible digital transformation.

My work connects academic research, technology innovation, policy development, and practical institutional solutions, with a particular interest in strengthening Africa’s capacity to develop and govern trustworthy digital technologies.

MY PROFESSIONAL VISION

To contribute to a digitally empowered Africa where technology innovation is secure, responsible, inclusive, and trusted.

MY MISSION
